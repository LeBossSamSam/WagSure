Parent / Guardian Approval

Because WagSure is run by a young pet sitter, every dog-sitting reservation is subject to parent/guardian approval.

This service is intended for shorter periods such as an evening or a few hours. It is not presented as long-term professional boarding, overnight professional care or veterinary care.
